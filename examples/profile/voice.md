# Voice (invented example)

How Alex writes. Onboarding starts this file from your answers and any writing samples you give it;
the `learn` skill adds a dated rule every time you edit a draft, so it gets more like you over time.

## Register
- Plain, warm and specific. Short paragraphs. Questions are real questions.

## Rules
- One credential per email at most; the middle paragraph is about the recipient's company.
- No em dashes.

## Banned phrases (never use)
- "I hope this email finds you well"
- "passionate about"
- "leverage my skills"
